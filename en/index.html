Frequently asked questions

Do I need a credit card to get started?

No. You get a 14-day trial with no card required to orchestrate your systems and try Orkestic with your business.

What happens after the 14 days?

If you haven't entered card details, you won't be charged automatically. If you'd like to continue, choose your plan and activate it from your account.

Do I need my own AI API key?

Yes. For now, you connect your own AI API key and pay the provider directly for your usage.

Can I use ChatGPT or Claude with Orkestic?

Yes. Orkestic's MCP connector lets you connect ChatGPT or Claude and work with your systems using natural-language instructions, depending on each platform's supported configuration.
