What each platform covers

Hootsuite provides social publishing, listening and approvals. Orkestic lets you connect the customer record to the actions you configure for this workflow.

The same job, two ways

A team prepares a post and publishes it to the right profile at a set time.

In Hootsuite

Connect the account, prepare the post and schedule it from the calendar.

In Orkestic

Connect the profile, upload the post, select the schedule and check its status after publication.

Connect an AI assistant

Both Orkestic plans include MCP. Depending on the connected tools and permissions, you can use an assistant to query data or prepare changes to agents, forms and workflows. Review the result with test data before going live.

Connect conversations to workflows

In Orkestic, a workflow can update a contact, apply a tag or start follow-up. If an agent starts the process, decide which fields it should collect and when to pass them into the workflow.

What to check after launch

Run the process end to end and check the result in the system that receives it. For this example, verify profile, format, time zone and live post. Check permissions, credentials and limits for any connected services.

When Hootsuite is the better choice

Listening, approval workflows and account management are useful for larger social teams.

When to consider Orkestic. Test this workflow in Orkestic if you want customer records, agent actions and follow-up to work together in one platform.

How to switch from Hootsuite to Orkestic

  1. Inventory what is live. Review profiles, roles and approvals.
  2. Prepare the data. Export the records you need where supported, map the fields and preserve contact preferences.
  3. Rebuild the priority process. Connect the profile, upload the post, select the schedule and check its status after publication.
  4. Test in parallel. Verify profile, format, time zone and live post before switching traffic.
  5. Switch the entry point. Enable the new workflow and retire the equivalent old process after you confirm it works.
