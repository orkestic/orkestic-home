Buyer guides

Orkestic, n8n, Zapier and Make

Start with the job. Zapier fits prebuilt app connections, Make suits visual branching, and n8n suits customization or self-hosting. If your job is to talk to leads, capture their details and follow up, test the journey in Orkestic.

n8n · Zapier · Make

How to choose a platform with MCP

Test a real task from the assistant you use. Ask it to inspect a form or build a test workflow. Check which actions are available, which account they run in and how you confirm the result.

Zapier · Make · n8n

How to choose an all-in-one AI platform

Follow a lead from a landing page to its first reply and handoff. Check where history lives, how the next step is assigned and what each plan includes.

GoHighLevel · HubSpot · Systeme.io

n8n alternatives for teams without developers

Recreate a workflow you already use and ask the operator to change one step. For external apps, assess connectors and error handling. If the workflow moves leads between forms, agents and CRM, try those steps in Orkestic.

n8n · Zapier · Make · Lindy

ManyChat alternatives for AI conversations

Test what happens when someone comments, asks a follow-up question and requests a booking. Compare channel rules, human handoff and where the lead is stored.

ManyChat · Chatfuel · Botpress · Voiceflow · CloseBot

GoHighLevel alternatives for businesses
