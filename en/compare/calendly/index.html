How to switch from Calendly to Orkestic

  1. Inventory what is live. Review meeting types, availability and routing rules.
  2. Prepare the data. Export the records you need where supported, map the fields and preserve contact preferences.
  3. Rebuild the priority process. Configure the agent calendar and availability, then verify that a test booking is recorded.
  4. Test in parallel. Verify availability, time zone and booking confirmation before switching traffic.
  5. Switch the entry point. Enable the new workflow and retire the equivalent old process after you confirm it works.
