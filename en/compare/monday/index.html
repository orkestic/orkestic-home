How to switch from monday.com to Orkestic

  1. Inventory what is live. Review boards, statuses and assignees.
  2. Prepare the data. Export the records you need where supported, map the fields and preserve contact preferences.
  3. Rebuild the priority process. Create a follow-up task from the customer record, assign it and verify that the owner can find the contact context.
  4. Test in parallel. Verify owner, due date and linked contact before switching traffic.
  5. Switch the entry point. Enable the new workflow and retire the equivalent old process after you confirm it works.
